# Bezubaan Complete Rescue SOPs

## Suspected Rabies or Severe Aggression
- **Symptoms:** Foaming, paralysis, unprovoked attacks, staggering.
- **Action:** DO NOT APPROACH. Warn bystanders. Priority is human safety.
- **Next Step:** Contact local animal control or police. Do not attempt rescue.

## Vehicular Trauma (Hit & Run)
- **Symptoms:** Immobility, bleeding, visibly broken bones.
- **Action:** Secure area from traffic. DO NOT move unless in immediate danger.
- **Handling:** Slide on a rigid board to prevent spinal injury. Do not bend.
- **Care:** Keep warm to prevent shock. DO NOT offer food/water (choking risk).

## Avian (Bird) Rescue
- **Action:** Drop a light towel over the bird. Pin wings to the body gently.
- **Housing:** Place in a dark, ventilated cardboard box. NO wire cages.
- **Care:** Keep quiet and warm. DO NOT force feed or give water (high aspiration risk).

## Heatstroke & Dehydration
- **Symptoms:** Excessive panting, drooling, dark red gums, collapse.
- **Action:** Move to shade immediately.
- **Cooling:** Apply cool (NOT ice) water to paws, belly, armpits.
- **Care:** Offer room-temp water if conscious. NO ice baths (causes shock).

## Severe Bleeding & Open Wounds
- **Action:** Approach cautiously. Apply firm, direct pressure with clean cloth.
- **Handling:** Do NOT lift cloth to check; add layers if it soaks through.
- **Next Step:** Elevate limb if safe. Immediate vet transport required.

## Maggot Wounds (Myiasis)
- **Action:** Do not attempt to manually dig out maggots.
- **Handling:** Keep the animal contained. Apply a light sterile gauze to prevent further fly strikes.
- **Next Step:** Requires professional vet cleaning and antibiotics.

## Suspected Poisoning
- **Symptoms:** Seizures, vomiting, severe diarrhea, foaming, lethargy.
- **Action:** Identify poison source if possible (bring packaging to vet).
- **Handling:** DO NOT induce vomiting unless instructed by a vet (some toxins burn the esophagus).
- **Care:** Immediate emergency transport.

## Snake or Insect Bites
- **Symptoms:** Sudden swelling (usually on face/legs), difficulty breathing, crying.
- **Action:** Keep the animal as calm and still as possible to slow venom spread.
- **Handling:** Do NOT apply a tourniquet. Do NOT attempt to suck venom.
- **Next Step:** Immediate anti-venom/vet administration.

## Abandoned Neonates (Kittens/Puppies)
- **Action:** OBSERVE first. The mother may be hunting. Wait 2-4 hours from a distance.
- **Handling:** If genuinely orphaned, keep them warm (body temp drops quickly).
- **Feeding:** DO NOT feed cow's milk (causes fatal diarrhea). Use KMR (Kitten/Puppy Milk Replacer) only.

## Electric Shock
- **Action:** Ensure power source is OFF before touching the animal.
- **Symptoms:** Burns on mouth (if chewed wire), unconsciousness, cardiac arrest.
- **Next Step:** Transport carefully; internal organ damage may not be visible.

## Choking
- **Symptoms:** Pawing at mouth, gasping, blue tongue.
- **Action:** Look inside mouth. Only remove object if easily visible and graspable.
- **Handling:** Do not push it further down. Perform modified Heimlich maneuver if trained, otherwise rush to vet.
